# Georgiamune

Georgiamune Inc. is a Gaithersburg, Maryland based biopharmaceutical company developing first-in-class immunotherapies that reprogram immune signaling pathways to rebalance the immune system across cancer and autoimmune diseases. Its target-discovery platform powers a pipeline of immunomodulators (including the clinical-stage antibody GIM-122, the Treg inhibitor GIM-531, and the Treg activator GIM-407) spanning clinical, preclinical, and discovery stages.

Website: https://georgiamune.com

Note: Georgiamune does not currently publish a public API, developer portal, or programmatic interface. This profile captures company identity and domain-security posture only. Surfaced as a venture-backed portfolio company (general-catalyst).
